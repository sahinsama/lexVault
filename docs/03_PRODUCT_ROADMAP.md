# Future Product Roadmap

This roadmap describes the long-term direction of LexVault. It is not a strict timeline but a collection of features and systems that represent the product vision. Priorities may change based on user feedback and future discoveries.

---

# 1. Personal Library

Every user owns a personal vocabulary library.

Features:

- Search
- Advanced filtering
- Favorites
- Mastered words
- Pending reviews
- Filter by source
- Filter by language
- Filter by type
- Filter by level
- Recently added
- Oldest words

---

# 2. Review System

A complete review system designed to maximize long-term retention.

Features:

- Daily reviews
- Weekly reviews
- Monthly reviews
- Spaced repetition
- AI-assisted quizzes
- "Do you actually remember this word?" sessions

---

# 3. Mastered Words

Words that have been truly learned should be separated from the rest of the library.

Possible requirements:

- Quiz success
- AI sentence evaluation
- Long-term successful reviews

---

# 4. AI Language Coach

AI becomes a teacher rather than a dictionary.

Responsibilities:

- Sentence evaluation
- Usage correction
- Natural wording suggestions
- Personalized explanations
- Quiz generation
- Learning reports

---

# 5. Quick Capture

One of LexVault's most important features.

Goal:

Capture a newly encountered word with as little friction as possible.

Planned platforms:

- Browser Extension
- Android Share Menu
- iOS Share Sheet
- Desktop Mini Popup

---

# 6. Statistics Dashboard

Users should be able to visualize their learning journey.

Examples:

- Total words
- Mastered words
- Monthly additions
- Daily average
- Most common sources
- Languages studied
- Current streak
- Contribution graph

---

# 7. Achievement System

Gamification should support learning without becoming distracting.

Examples:

- First 100 words
- First mastered word
- 30-day streak
- First AI sentence
- 100 completed reviews

---

# 8. Friends

Users can connect with friends.

Possible features:

- Friend profiles
- Shared vocabulary
- Words learned from friends
- Common interests

---

# 9. Community

Community-driven discovery.

Examples:

- Trending words
- Most added words this week
- Popular sources
- Shared notes
- Community collections

---

# 10. Multi-Language Support

LexVault is not limited to English.

Users select their native language during onboarding.

Captured words may belong to any language.

Examples:

- English
- Japanese
- German
- French
- Spanish

Definitions are always presented in the user's native language.

---

# 11. Integrations

Long-term integrations include:

- Notion
- Obsidian
- Anki
- Kindle
- Readwise
- Browser Extension

Notion becomes an optional synchronization target.

LexVault remains the primary database.

---

# 12. Mobile Widgets

Widgets designed to keep users connected with their vocabulary.

Examples:

- Daily streak
- Reviews due today
- Random mastered word
- Contribution graph

---

# 13. Premium

The free version should always remain fully usable.

Premium features may include:

- AI Language Coach
- Advanced statistics
- Personalized reports
- Adaptive quizzes
- Unlimited AI-powered analysis

---

# 14. Long-Term Goal

LexVault is not intended to become another language course platform.

Its purpose is to become a lifelong vocabulary companion.

Users should be able to capture, organize, review, and truly master every meaningful word they encounter, regardless of the language or where they found it.

The ultimate vision is to build a personal language ecosystem that grows naturally alongside the user's life.

# North Star

> **Build the world's most natural way to capture, remember and truly master vocabulary encountered in everyday life.**

Every major product decision should support this goal.

If a feature does not improve capturing, remembering, or mastering vocabulary, it should be reconsidered.

---

# Vision

## What is LexVault?

LexVault is a vocabulary learning platform built around one simple belief:

> **People remember words they genuinely encounter far better than words they are forced to memorize.**

Traditional language-learning applications begin by presenting vocabulary lists, structured lessons, or predefined learning paths.

LexVault takes the opposite approach.

Instead of asking users to study words chosen by the application, LexVault allows them to preserve the words that naturally spark their curiosity.

Those words may come from:

- Movies
- TV Shows
- Books
- Games
- YouTube videos
- Wikipedia articles
- Conversations
- Social media
- Any other real-world experience

Every day people discover unfamiliar words.

Most of those words disappear forever—not because they are difficult, but because capturing them is inconvenient.

LexVault exists to eliminate that friction.

Rather than becoming another language course, LexVault becomes the user's personal vocabulary memory.

---

# The Problem

Most language learners experience the same cycle.

1. Encounter an unfamiliar word.
2. Become curious.
3. Open Google Translate.
4. Read the definition.
5. Forget the word a few minutes later.

Users who attempt to build a vocabulary habit often create systems that eventually become difficult to maintain.

Examples include:

- Random Notes pages
- Scattered Notion databases
- Screenshots
- Browser bookmarks
- Paper notebooks
- Abandoned Anki decks

The problem is not motivation.

The problem is friction.

Every additional step between discovering a word and saving it dramatically reduces the likelihood that the word will ever be reviewed again.

---

# Mission

LexVault's mission is **not** to teach languages.

Its mission is to:

> **Capture curiosity before it disappears.**

The platform should become the fastest place to save a word and the best place to rediscover it months or even years later.

---

# Product Identity

LexVault is **not**:

- Duolingo
- Babbel
- Memrise
- A grammar course
- A flashcard application

LexVault is closer to being:

> **A personal language operating system.**

Its purpose is to preserve every meaningful word a person encounters throughout life.

Instead of asking:

> "What lesson would you like to study today?"

LexVault asks:

> **"What caught your attention today?"**

That single question defines the entire product philosophy.

# Product Principles

Every future feature should respect these principles.

## Principle 1 — Real Life Is The Curriculum

Books, movies, conversations and the internet already provide an infinite language course.

LexVault should never attempt to replace them.

It should simply preserve what users naturally discover.

---

## Principle 2 — Capture Before Organization

Fast capture is more valuable than perfect organization.

A word saved without tags is infinitely more valuable than the perfectly categorized word that was never saved.

---

## Principle 3 — Users Own Their Memories

AI may generate explanations.

AI may explain grammar.

AI may generate quizzes.

However, AI should never replace the user's own mental connections.

The **Notes** section always belongs to the user.

---

## Principle 4 — AI Is A Teacher

AI is an assistant.

It is not the product.

The application should remain valuable even if AI is temporarily unavailable.

Dictionary data, personal notes, review history and user context are the true long-term assets.

---

## Principle 5 — Every Feature Must Improve Retention

Features should never exist simply because they are interesting.

Every new addition must improve at least one of the following:

- Capture speed
- Long-term retention
- User motivation
- Learning quality

If it does none of these, it should be reconsidered.

---

# Long-Term Vision

If LexVault succeeds, users should eventually feel uncomfortable learning languages without it.

It should become the first place they think of whenever they encounter something worth remembering.

Not because the application forces them to study.

But because it naturally becomes part of how they experience the internet itself.
